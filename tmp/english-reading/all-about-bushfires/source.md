# All About Bushfires

## Article

### P01

Bushfires are fires that burn in the bush. These fires are common in hot and dry places, like Australia, Africa and parts of the US.

### P02

Bushfires can start naturally, such as when lightning strikes dry plants. Believe it or not, bushfires are part of a natural cycle.

### P03

When a bushfire starts, it burns grass and kills insects that are bad for trees. It makes more space for new plants to grow. It makes more space for sunlight to get to the ground too. This is good for animals because they can find new food and make new homes.

### P04

Sadly, bushfires can be started by people. For example, a bushfire can start when someone hasn't correctly put out a campfire.

### P05

Climate change doesn't start bushfires, but it makes them larger. That's because hotter summers and stronger winds make it easy for fires to get started and to spread. This makes it harder for firefighters to put out these fires.
